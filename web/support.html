VibeRadar Support

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Need help with VibeRadar, your subscription, account deletion, pairing, or App Store review access? Contact us and include your device, app version, and a short description of the issue.

Contact

Email: support@viberadar.app

Account and Data

  • Delete your account from VibeRadar Settings using Delete account.
  • For data questions, email support with the subject “Privacy Request”.
  • For subscription issues, use Apple’s purchase management tools and include the product id if you contact us.
