VibeRadar Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

VibeRadar (“we”, “us”) is a DJ trend-intelligence and set-building app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using VibeRadar you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we receive your email address, display name, and provider ID to create and secure your account. You may also use the app as an anonymous guest on desktop, which creates an ID without personal details.
  • Content you create — crates/setlists, profile details, and any audio or artwork you upload to the community feature.
  • Preferences and activity — region, followed artists, watchlist, likes, and follows, used to personalize trends and recommendations.
  • AI prompts — messages you send to the in-app AI Copilot are sent to our processor (OpenAI) to generate a response. We do not use them to train models.
  • Device pairing — a temporary code links your phone and desktop so setlists can sync between them.

How we use information

  • To provide, sync, and secure the app across your devices.
  • To generate AI set-building suggestions you request.
  • To power community features (profiles, uploads, follows) that you opt into.
  • To show trending tracks aggregated from public music-platform data.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, storage, and backend functions. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI — processes your AI Copilot prompts to return responses.
  • Music data providers (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Beatport) — we read public trend/chart data via their APIs; we do not share your personal data with them.

What is public

If you use the community feature, your public profile and the content you choose to upload are visible to other users. Everything else (your crates, preferences, AI history) is private to your account.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, uploads, crates, social connections, and pairings from our systems.

Children

VibeRadar is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase’s security controls. API keys are kept on our backend, never in the app.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@viberadar.app.
